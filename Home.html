Welcome to the AI-Assisted Symptom Checker

This project aims to provide users a quick and intuitive way to log their symptoms and receive possible next steps based on rule-based analysis and AI-driven suggestions. It is designed to help individuals understand potential health conditions and encourage timely medical consultation.

Enter your symptoms, such as fever, cough, headache, or sore throat, and get suggestions on what actions to take next. This system is meant for **informational purposes only** and does not replace professional medical advice.
